# LeafGust

Layout's LeafGust (Ken, 2026-10-31): Canopy's `LeafGust.cs`, the wind in the LeafSplit leaves: a steady wind plus gusts, each still, light, medium or strong (☰ View Wind, Gusts), and up to three cylinder gusts rolling through in front of the camera; run by its stand-in, `layout/scripts/LeafGust.js`. The Wind's lines follow it. The cube is what you tap; where it stands doesn't matter. One at a time (a singleton). Settings on a piece placing this change its params (e.g. `radius 30, speed 6, interval 6`).

- Shape: box 1 1 1 color #d8cfa8
- Script: scripts/LeafGust.js
