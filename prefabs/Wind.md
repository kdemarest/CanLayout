# Wind

Layout's Wind (Ken, 2026-10-29): an experiment, not in Canopy. `layout/pfx/Wind.js` draws lines wafting past the camera where the leaves' wind is: a few along the steady wind, more inside each gust, travelling with it (it follows LeafGust). The cube is what you tap; where it stands doesn't matter. One wind at a time (a singleton). Settings on the Script line, or on a piece placing this, change its params (e.g. `rate 2, speed 4`).

- Shape: box 1 1 1 color #e8e8e8
- Script: pfx/Wind.js
