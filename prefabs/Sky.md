# Sky

Layout's Sky (Ken, 2026-10-29): Canopy's sky (Game.unity's CanSky skybox and EnvironmentManager's sky globals), drawn by `layout/scripts/Skybox.js` from the scene's `unity.json` environment. The cube is what you tap; where it stands doesn't matter. One sky at a time (a singleton). Without one the background is plain.

- Shape: box 1 1 1 color #87a9c9
- Script: scripts/Skybox.js
